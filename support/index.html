Support

Getting Gradial to do what you want.

There is no account to recover and no subscription to cancel, so most support questions here are about what the app needs in order to fill itself in.

Get in touch

Email support@enalmar.uk, or open an issue at github.com/enalmar/gradial-site/issues. It is the fastest route and it is public, so the next person with the same question finds the answer. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you are on, and what you expected to happen.

If the problem involves your health data, do not paste it into an issue. Describe the shape of the problem instead: nothing about your runs ever needs to leave your devices to get this fixed.

What the app needs

  • An iPhone running iOS 26 or later, an iPad on iPadOS 26, or a Mac on macOS 26. There is no build for earlier versions.
  • Apple Health, for reading your finished runs. The plan works without it; the scoring does not.
  • An Apple Watch running watchOS 26 or later, if you want the sessions on your wrist. Everything else works without one.

Common questions

My runs are not appearing

Gradial reads finished runs from Apple Health, so the run has to be in Health first. Check Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Gradial and confirm that Workouts, Workout Routes and Heart Rate are on. iOS only asks for permission once, so if you declined the first time, this is where you change it.

A run is there but it did not tick off a session

A run is matched to the session it most plausibly satisfied, on date and distance. A run three days from the planned day, or less than about two thirds of the distance asked for, will not be matched. You can look at any week to see which sessions were matched and which were not.

My race prediction says it is provisional

Predictions are built from Apple Health's VO₂max, which Health only writes after a few outdoor runs with heart rate on an Apple Watch. Until it has one, Gradial predicts from the race time you entered when you built the plan, and says so rather than showing a confident number it cannot support.

Sending to my watch does nothing

Scheduling needs a paired Apple Watch on watchOS 26 or later, and the first send asks the watch for permission. If no watch is paired the button says so. Sessions appear in the Workout app on the watch on the day they are scheduled.

The paces feel wrong

Two levers. On the Plan tab, the fitness check offers to re-anchor the whole plan to your measured fitness. Inside it, Adjust this plan lets you nudge every pace or the weekly volume by a percentage, or schedule a time trial and anchor to the result.

How do I withdraw Health access?

Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Gradial, and turn off whatever you want to withhold. The app keeps working and says which parts have gone quiet. To remove everything Gradial has stored, delete the app: the plan file goes with it, and your Health data was never copied out in the first place.

Can I move my plan to a new phone, or see it on my iPad or Mac?

With Gradial Sync, yes: buy it once, sign in to iCloud on each device, and your plans and runs are copied between them. Runs are recorded on the iPhone and arrive on the others. Without it, nothing leaves the phone: your runs live in Apple Health and travel as Health travels; the plan itself does not, and you would build it again.

I bought Sync and my other device says it is not.

Open You, Sync on that device and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is on, so a family member's purchase counts too. If the App Store cannot be reached the section says so and offers to try again.

My iPad or Mac shows no runs.

Runs arrive from the iPhone, so three things need to be true: Sync is bought, the iPhone has opened Gradial since (it writes the runs down in the background, newest first, and shows its progress under You, Sync), and both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account. Sync now, in the same section, asks iCloud again.

Known limitations

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple Health is on the iPhone only, so an iPad or Mac without an iPhone and Gradial Sync shows a plan but no runs. No Vision Pro version.
  • Not available in the European Union at launch.
  • Watch scheduling cannot be tested without a physical Apple Watch.
  • Running dynamics (cadence, ground contact, vertical oscillation, stride length) only appear where your watch recorded them.
  • Gradial is training software. It does not diagnose anything, and it has no opinion on whether you are well enough to run.
