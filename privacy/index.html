Privacy policy

What Gradial reads, and where it goes.

Gradial keeps your data on your devices. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics. If you buy Gradial Pro, your plans and runs are copied between your devices through your own iCloud, and nowhere else.

Last updated 11 November 2026. This page is the same policy shown inside the app under You, Privacy. Where the two ever disagree, the one in the app is correct.

What Gradial reads from Health

Only to build and score your plan, and only on this device:

  • Running workouts: distance, duration, date, and energy burned.
  • Workout routes: the GPS trace recorded with a run.
  • Heart rate during runs, and your resting heart rate.
  • Cardio fitness (VO₂max), which every race prediction is built from.
  • Steps and walking or running distance.
  • Running power, ground contact time, vertical oscillation and stride length, where your watch records them.
  • Date of birth and biological sex, from Health's own profile, used only to place your VO₂max against the published norms for people of the same age and sex. Nothing is shown if Health does not hold them.

What Gradial writes to Health

Planned running workouts, so your Apple Watch can show the session and its steps. Nothing else is ever written.

Location

Route data is read from the workouts Health already holds, to draw the map of a run and measure its distance. Gradial does not track your location itself, does not run in the background, and never sends a coordinate anywhere but, with sync, your own iCloud. Earlier versions looked up a place name from the start of each run, and that lookup has been removed for exactly this reason.

Where it is kept

Plans, the links between runs and sessions, and the marks you make on them live in a single file in the app's own storage, plus a small copy shared with the widget. On an iPhone, Health data is not copied out of Health; it is read when a screen needs it. Deleting Gradial deletes all of it.

Sync

Sync is part of Gradial Pro, a one-off purchase. With it, your iPhone writes each run down — the route, the heart-rate trace, the pace and the form figures the watch recorded — and your plans, your heart-rate settings and your runs are copied between your devices through your own iCloud account, in the app's private area of it. Apple holds that data under your Apple Account; we cannot read it, and no one else can. It is not used for anything but showing you your own runs.

  • Nothing is sent until sync is bought and the device is signed in to iCloud.
  • Remove from iCloud, in the app's Sync section, deletes everything sync put there.
  • Signing out of iCloud on an iPad or Mac clears the runs it received; the plans stay.
  • The purchase itself is handled by the App Store. Gradial never sees a payment detail.

Studio

Studio never talks to an AI itself. When you copy a prompt, it holds your plan and a summary of your last four weeks of running — dates, distances, paces, heart rate — and it goes only where you paste it, under that service's own terms. Gradial also keeps a folder named Gradial in your iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud: your active plan, your runs since the plan began (date, distance, duration, pace, average heart rate, and how each matched its session), and a note explaining the format, so that an AI on your Mac that you give access to can read them. Gradial reads back only the plan files, and changes nothing until you have reviewed and applied the change.

  • No route or location is written to the folder.
  • Deleting the folder in Files or Finder is safe; Gradial writes it again the next time it opens.
  • Turning iCloud Drive off for Gradial stops the folder; copying and pasting a prompt keep working.

What is never collected

  • No account, email address or name.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • Nothing shared with anyone. Your data goes only to your own iCloud — sync, if you buy it, and the Studio folder in iCloud Drive — and wherever you paste a prompt yourself.

Your control

Health access can be withdrawn at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Gradial. The app keeps working; the parts that need your runs stop filling in, and say so. iCloud can be turned off for Gradial in your Apple Account's iCloud settings, or its data removed from within the app.

Children

Gradial collects nothing from anyone, of any age, because it collects nothing at all. Sync and Studio store your data in your own iCloud and nowhere we can see.
